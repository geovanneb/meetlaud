# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** open a [discussion](../../discussions). English, Japanese, Portuguese, and Spanish are all fine.
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose) with the bug template. Please include your device model, iOS version, and app version, and leave out any real meeting content.
- **Security or privacy problems:** do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Meetlaud has no account and no server, so we cannot see your recordings or your settings. That also means we cannot recover anything for you: keep an exported backup if your meetings matter.
